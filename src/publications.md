## Book chapter and journal article

- Boulnois, S., & Quillerou, E. (2022). Managing and preventing operational risk-taking at design time: Stories from a human-centered design project. In *Risk-Taking, Prevention and Design* (pp. 185–212). CRC Press.
- Quillerou, E., & Boulnois, S. (2021). Analysing human activity development through (work) spaces in a human-centered design project for commercial airline pilots. *Journal of Workplace Learning*, 33(1), 62–80.

## Conference papers
